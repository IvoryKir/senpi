### Added

- Durable core: the Transport contract (opaque frames on named lanes with per-lane order, exactly-once delivery, resume from each lane's last ACK after the carrier re-attaches, and per-lane backpressure) with a carrier seam, a reference transport over an in-process carrier addressed by local endpoints (owner-only Unix sockets on POSIX, named pipes on Windows), and the TRANSPORT-01..03, MULTI-LANE-01, MULTI-FAIR-01 and FAULT-TRANSPORT-01..05 conformance cases with five deterministic fault profiles, exported with the adapter types from `@earendil-works/pi-agent-core/conformance` ([#4741](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4741), fixes [#4738](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/4738)).
