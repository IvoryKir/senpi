## 2026-10-29 - A models.json baseUrl wins over an extension-registered provider's baseUrl (senpi#4765)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/provider-base-url-override.ts` (new): `applyUserBaseUrl` is the last baseUrl-affecting composition step. For every model (chat, image, classifier) of a provider with a models.json entry it applies models.json `models[i].baseUrl` (chat models), else the provider `baseUrl`, over the extension's per-model and provider `baseUrl`, a native provider's model `baseUrl`, and `oauth.modifyModels` output. One decision function classifies each model as `none` / `apply` / `withheld`; `withheld` (the owner declared a different baseUrl, serves the model through its own `streamSimple`/`images`/`classifiers` or native transport, and did not declare `honorsModelBaseUrl`) keeps today's destination.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/provider-composer.ts`: `ProviderConfigInput.honorsModelBaseUrl`; `composeModelProvider` accepts `options.baseOrigin` ("builtin" | "native-extension", default "builtin") and runs `applyUserBaseUrl` after `applyExtension` and after `oauth.modifyModels` in `getAllModels`, in `validateExtensionProvider`, and in the refresh-publish validation; the composed `Provider.baseUrl` now prefers the models.json `baseUrl` over the extension's.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-runtime.ts`: `composeProvider` passes `baseOrigin: "native-extension"` when the base is a provider registered through `registerProvider(provider: Provider)`, else `"builtin"`.

### Why

- senpi#4765: with an extension-registered provider, a user's models.json `baseUrl` (a proxy, gateway or test server) was ignored and requests, with the configured credential, still went to the extension's hard-coded host. `applyExtension` overwrote every model with the extension baseUrl and `extensionModelFromDefinition` ranked the user's value lowest.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Precedence between models.json and extension registrations is decided inside the provider composer; an extension cannot reorder composition layers it is itself one of.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `getAllModels`, `validateExtensionProvider` and the refresh-publish `update()` in `provider-composer.ts` (each site now computes `baseModels` and wraps `applyExtension` in `applyUserBaseUrl`), and the `Provider.baseUrl` line of the composed provider literal.
- LOW: `composeProvider` in `model-runtime.ts` (`nativeBase` local and the `baseOrigin` option).

## 2026-10-29 - `ProviderConfig.honorsModelBaseUrl` and the models.json baseUrl caveat (senpi#4765)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `ProviderConfig` gains `honorsModelBaseUrl?: boolean`, declaring that `streamSimple`, `images` and `classifiers` send requests to `model.baseUrl`; the `registerProvider` and `ProviderConfig.baseUrl` docs state that a models.json `baseUrl` (provider or `models[i]`) takes precedence over the registration's.

### Why

- senpi#4765: a models.json baseUrl now wins over the extension's. The composer cannot tell whether a custom transport reads `model.baseUrl`, so an extension that does declares it; undeclared custom transports keep their own baseUrl instead of being silently pointed at a URL they would ignore.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The flag is part of the public registration contract the composer reads; extensions cannot extend `ProviderConfig`.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `registerProvider` doc block and the `ProviderConfig` field list near `streamSimple` in `types.ts`.

## 2026-10-29 - `Provider.honorsModelBaseUrl` (senpi#4765)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/models.ts`: `Provider` gains optional `readonly honorsModelBaseUrl?: boolean`, declaring that the provider's own transports send each request to `model.baseUrl`.

### Why

- senpi#4765: senpi applies a user's models.json baseUrl over an extension-registered provider's. For a native `Provider` registered by an extension, the composer trusts its transport with the override only when it declares this flag.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `Provider` is the pi-ai public provider type; a native-provider declaration has to live on it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `Provider` interface field list after `baseUrl` in `models.ts`.
