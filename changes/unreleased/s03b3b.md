## 2026-10-27 - Journal the cold message flows at their producers; keep a capture failure off the legacy path (S03b-3b, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-journal.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-flows.ts`: while a consumer journals the transcript, a context edit records an `edit` flow (the target's edited message) or, with no replacement, a `delete` flow; a compaction records the messages it took out of the model context; a leaf move (`branch`, `resetLeaf`, `branchWithSummary`) records the messages that left the model context and the ones that joined it, as owned JSON. Nothing is computed when no journal exists.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/structured-session-lane.ts`: the provider-capture copy of a structured record runs inside the structured failure boundary; its throw is reported to the structured listener's `failed` callback and costs only that record (#4439 delta LOW-1).

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-journal.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-flows.ts`: RULING-33 condition 2 requires the four flows that target settled messages (edit, user delete, compaction removal, branch) to come from their real producers, ordered after the accepted transcript change.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/structured-session-lane.ts`: the structured lane runs before the legacy listeners, so a capture throw would have stopped legacy and extension delivery of the event.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/structured-session-lane.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-journal.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-flows.ts` own the manager's committed-transcript journal and the session's private emission order; extensions see neither.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts`: `_appendEntry`, `appendContextEdit`, `appendCompaction`, `branch`, `resetLeaf`, `branchWithSummary`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `subscribeStructured`. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/structured-session-lane.ts`: `subscribe` and `emitEvent`. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-transcript-journal.ts`: the `TranscriptTransition` union.

## 2026-10-27 - Frozen lifecycle and dialog roots, producer-driven cold flows and persistence status for compact state (S03b-3b, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-current-components.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-session-state.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-state-view.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transition.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-acceptance-lane.ts`: run state, the latest `session_lifecycle` and the open dialogs are persistent roots of the compact cut, each change at its own lane sequence. A broadcast question keeps its request; a targeted dialog keeps only its id, method and deadline, so a cut never holds a body only its authorized path may present (RULING-25 Q4, RULING-26 Q4).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transcript-flows.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-lane-current.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-settled-messages.ts`: each journaled flow maps through this epoch's settled joins (entry id to message id, one cut) to `applyColdFlow`; persistence status (`appended` from an owned append or a pre-committed terminal, `failed` from `transcript_write_failed`) and the flows run on one ordered cold chain after acceptance. A failed cold write makes the epoch's compact state unavailable.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-settled-messages.ts`: carried item 9. A message both ended and joined leaves the resident key set; only unjoined terminals and appends that preceded their end keep keys. The `ended` marker rows are gone.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker.ts`: one `canonical_unavailable` frame per epoch, reset at a replacement (#4439 delta LOW-2). `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/structured-session-records.ts`: the capture failure boundary is passed to the lane (LOW-1).

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-current-components.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transcript-flows.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-lane-current.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-session-state.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-settled-messages.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-state-view.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transition.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-acceptance-lane.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/structured-session-records.ts`: RULING-26 Q4 (frozen lifecycle/dialog roots before any retention removal), RULING-25 Q4 (targeted dialogs only on authorized paths), RULING-33 condition 2 (cold flows ordered after acceptance), carried item 9 (settled membership grew for the whole epoch), #4439 delta LOWs 1-3.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-current-components.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transcript-flows.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-lane-current.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-session-state.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-settled-messages.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-state-view.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-transition.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-acceptance-lane.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/structured-session-records.ts` are the host's internal acceptance lane and compact consumer; no extension API reaches them.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-acceptance-lane.ts`: `SEQUENCED`, `applyTranscript`, `accept`. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-session-state.ts`: `accept`, `freeze`. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-settled-messages.ts`: `end`, `join`. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker.ts`: `canonicalUnavailable`.
